SoleCraft in the Apple Store style

Second draft of all 18 screens, plus a new Developers page. It keeps your colors, and your red does the job Apple's blue does: primary buttons, links, and the New and Sale labels. Products, prices, form fields and page copy are real, taken from your catalog and app. Orders, notifications and reviews show example rows. Nothing has been pushed to FlutterFlow yet.

One white group per step, the way the Apple Store checkout reads. Place Order stays pinned to the bottom.

Decisions for you

  • Secondary text gets darker. Your warm grey #8A8578 is 3.7:1 on white, which is too faint for small text. #6E6A60 is the same hue at 5.4:1. The old grey stays for icons and borders.
  • One new tone, Mist. Paper #FAF9F6 is almost identical to white, so tiles and grouped screens need a step between paper and stone.
  • For your info: white on your red is 4.2:1, about the same as Apple's own blue buttons (4.0:1). Fine for bold button labels.
